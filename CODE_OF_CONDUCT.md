# Code of Conduct

Welcome to the CC0-A project! We hope everyone here can communicate in a friendly and helpful manner.

## What we hope for

- Be kind and respectful to others
- Communicate constructively when there are different opinions
- If you have questions, feel free to reach out

## What we don't want

- Insults or personal attacks
- Spam or off-topic content
- Intentional disruption

If you encounter any issues, please contact the developer: xiaoliwu18614299510@outlook.com
